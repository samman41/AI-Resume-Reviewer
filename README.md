ResuMate AI

A resume analyzer that checks how well your resume matches a job description. Upload your resume (PDF/DOCX), paste the job description, and it uses Gemini to score the match, point out missing keywords, and rewrite weak bullet points.

Live: [https://ai-resume-reviewer-pcd8.onrender.com]

What it does
Scores your resume against a job description (0-100)
Breaks it down by skills, experience, formatting, education
Shows which keywords from the JD are missing
Rewrites your bullet points to sound better
Gives you a step-by-step checklist to improve your score
Stack

FastAPI + Gemini 2.5 Flash on the backend, plain HTML/CSS/JS on the frontend.
