How the ATS Optimizer Works

Most modern employers use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to filter out resumes before a human recruiter ever sees them. Here is how ResuMate AI helps you bypass these digital gatekeepers:

1. Document Processing

Extracts text metadata directly from PDF and MS Word documents without breaking formatting characters.

2. Semantic Matching

Utilizes Gemini's advanced LLM capacity to analyze contextual meaning rather than raw keyword stuffing.

3. Multi-Dimension Scoring

Grades formatting readability, educational match, skills alignment, and structural metrics.
